## About

I'm a junior **AI & Data Engineer** who works across the full data lifecycle — designing scalable pipelines, training and evaluating models, and shipping them reliably to production. I care about systems that are reproducible, observable, and built to last.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** End-to-end AI solutions — automated data pipelines powering production ML models
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Generative AI architectures, real-time streaming frameworks, and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform)
- 👯 **Looking to collaborate on:** Open-source projects at the intersection of Data Engineering, MLOps, and AI
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Apache Spark, Airflow orchestration, ML model deployment, and RAG systems
